# exp3ndable

New to flying? Choose **Tutorial** on the start screen (**Übung** in German).
It is optional and replayable: explanations stop the flight, then you practise
holding, releasing, collecting and avoiding a wall with the normal controls.
Back, skip and leave are available; practice never enters scores or saved runs.
It never starts automatically, including after an update or cleared browser data.

**You were only going to get coffee.**

A mellow sci-fi comedy runner about a very junior officer having a very long shift.

Strap on a jetpack. Dodge questionable machinery. Collect salvage. Borrow a shuttle
that probably passed inspection in another dimension.

**Hold to fly. Release to fall. Tap to fire.** That's your entire job description.

## Welcome aboard

- **Six places to be underqualified.** Your ship, Compliance Core, Gut Transit,
  Pocket Dimension, Pelagic Annex, and Warranty Moon. Each has its own scenery,
  creatures, music, and a slightly different pull of gravity.
- **Portals are optional.** Fly through to travel, or go above or below to keep
  your current surroundings. Pause while a portal approaches to read its destination
  and routes at your own pace. The upper half gives low gravity; the lower half
  doubles salvage for a short time. You and your equipment cross; fired moons,
  enemies, and loose supplies stay behind.
- **Eight species with jobs to avoid.** Floating auditors, escaped rations,
  three-eyed rays, coffee spores, scrap crabs, jellies, parcels with teeth,
  and union cleaners. Each dimension has its own cast, with occasional squabbling pairs.
- **Stillmatter holds time together.** Collect the gold bits, energy cells,
  data cubes, and odd relics to lower the **Time debt** meter. Miss too much and
  the corridor gradually speeds up. Regular collecting keeps a mellow cruise;
  you don't need every piece. Every 50 collected credits earns a tiny woohoo.
- **Six very bad ideas.** Fast ion bolts, a three-photon-torpedo spread, a thick
  amber beam ahead, chaining Arc fork electricity, a six-shot biting
  Pet Moon with nervous, angry, blinking faces, or five rounds of Red Tape. Empty special
  weapons hand you back the rifle. The paperwork says DENIED. It means it. Moons and stamps alternate in a rare
  pickup slot, once every eight offers.
- **One extremely borrowed shuttle.** Three hull points and a spectacular breakup.
  Pickups mount stronger weapons: five-torpedo broadsides, a wider amber cannon,
  five-target lightning, paired moons,
  or a bigger stamp. One tap spends one charge; ejection keeps the rest and your
  crew health. It handles a little heavier in each dimension, with enough
  lift to fly everywhere.
- **Mistakes are recoverable.** Three health points, medical pickups, a brief
  recovery shield, and a pace that eases up to a ceiling.
- **A crew with opinions.** Anxious Pip, deadpan Siv, and recklessly scientific
  Moss save their conversations for breaks. Flight stays clear of popup messages;
  pickups and hits use small visual cues, sounds, and the existing meters.
  Workplaces tell their stories through slow visual comedy: giant coffee cups,
  overqualified stamping machines, levitating office furniture and fish with desks.
  There is no need to read background signs or interact with the scenery.

## Controls

| Action | Touch / mouse | Keyboard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Start or resume | Tap **Play**, **Again**, or **Resume** | Press **Space**, **↑**, or **W** |
| Fly and fire | Hold / tap the **Fly** pad or flight area | Hold **Space**; each new press fires |
| Fly without firing | — | Hold **↑** or **W** |
| Fall | Release | Release the flight key |
| Fire | Tap / click the **Fly** pad or flight area | Tap **X** |
| Restart a paused run | Pause, tap **Restart**, then confirm | **Tab** to **Restart**, **Enter**, then confirm |
| Pause / resume | Tap the pause button / **Resume** | Press **P** or **Esc** |
| Music & sound | Tap the speaker button | Focus the speaker button with **Tab**, then press **Enter** |
| Fullscreen | Tap the fullscreen button, when available | Focus it with **Tab**, then press **Enter** |
| Pilot name & scores | Tap your name / **Scores**; enter a name and tap **Done** | **Tab** through the form; **Enter** saves; **Esc** closes Scores |

Each touch or mouse press also fires a shot. Holding keeps you climbing; it doesn't
keep firing. **Space** behaves the same way. **↑** and **W** fly without shooting or spending ammo;
**X** fires without changing your flight.

Use **Tab** and **Enter** for menu buttons. When the **Fly** button itself has
keyboard focus, holding **Enter** also flies and fires once on press. Name entry keeps normal typing.

## Your shift report

Every ending includes time on duty, where your shift ended, and all Stillmatter
gathered during that run—even if damage cost you some afterward. The crew's
sign-off changes with your haul and final dimension. The coffee remains unresolved.
Saved and resumed shifts keep the same facts for their report.

## Surviving your shift

**Choose your language.** Use English or Deutsch on the start or Pause screen. English is the default; your choice is remembered on this device and does not affect your saved shift.

**Choose your shift.** Regular is the more forgiving default. Hard keeps the
tougher time-pressure balance. Your choice is remembered for the next run;
scores and personal bests are separate. Earlier scores are kept under Hard.

**Watch the corridor.** Touching the floor or ceiling is harmless, but walking
won't dodge bulkheads or creatures. Fly around them; keep gathering Stillmatter
to settle the time meter. You don't need to collect every trail.

**Take the supplies.** Stillmatter, medical packets, weapons, shuttles, and portals
are immune to your gunfire. Fly into them to collect or enter them.

**Borrow the big guns.** Photon spreads and Arc forks last twelve volleys, Amber
lances eight, Pet Moons six, and Red Tape five. Shuttle upgrades spend one charge
per volley too. When they run out, the unlimited rifle or shuttle emitter returns;
another special comes along after a stretch of ordinary flying.

**A hit isn't the end.** On foot, it costs one health point, a quarter of your
salvage rounded up, and your weapon. The brief shield gives you time to recover.
Medical packets restore one health point, up to three. Shuttle hits use hull
instead, protecting your health, salvage, carried weapon, and remaining charges.

**Follow a trail.** Twelve gently curving formations give you different routes.
Cells contain two credits and grant a brief shield. Data cubes hold three credits
and briefly pause time-debt growth. Relics hold five and repair a hull or
health point; when fully repaired they grant a longer shield. Gold bits hold one
credit. They all stabilize time, working harder when debt is high. Double salvage
boosts your score without doubling time relief. Missing a stretch can be recovered from, and speed changes
blend smoothly. Flight response and firing keep pace with the world.

**Borrowed tools.** Guns and shuttle mounts wear taped repairs and service cables.
Instant beams and paperwork have a finite forward reach; a wider screen provides
more preview without an unlimited-range attack.

**Read the room.** Regular fire cannot clear solid bulkheads. A moon or Red Tape
has other ideas. The amber lance can cut through with repeated shots. You can stay in a world you like by flying around the next portal. Nobody is checking
whether this was the approved route.

## Make yourself comfortable

- **Sound starts with Play.** Music, the air jet, and effects are enabled by default.
  The speaker button remembers your mute choice. Music changes when you cross
  into a different dimension. On iPhone, turn Silent Mode off to hear the game.
- **Portrait or landscape.** Scenery fills the screen. HUD and menus stay clear of
  cutouts; a small translucent corner control leaves the world visible. You can
  hold elsewhere in the flight area too, or use a mouse or keyboard. Rotating pauses
  your shift; tap **Resume** to continue.
- **Your name, your scores.** Pick a pilot name and chase your personal best.
  Your name and top five runs in each mode stay in that browser; there is no shared leaderboard.
- **Leave a shift, come back to it.** Pausing or switching away saves your active run.
  Reopening returns to the pause screen with your gear, health and progress intact;
  tap **Resume** when ready, or **Restart** to begin a fresh shift in the same mode.
  Restart asks for confirmation, then replaces the saved run and keeps your name,
  scores and sound preference.
  Flight also saves every three seconds as a fallback.
  Saves stay on that device and site; clearing browser data removes them.
- **Take it offline.** After an online visit has finished loading, the game can
  reopen offline in a compatible browser. Add it to your Home Screen where supported;
  the app includes a dedicated iOS touch icon and raster install icons.
- **Updates arrive automatically.** While online, the app downloads a complete
  release before reloading. Your shift is saved and reopens paused. Failed or
  offline downloads keep the installed version; keep your site data to keep saves.
